No. 02Before you book

Good to know.

Is hair included?

Yes on most styles. Knotless, boho, bob, island twist, Fulani and cornrow prices on the menu include hair. Soft locs are priced with hair not included. Not sure? Ask when you text Mimi.

How do I book?

Use the booking request on this site, text or call (469) 441-9208, or DM @braid_by_mimi on Instagram. There is no app to download.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A $25 deposit holds your appointment. Mimi confirms your day and time by text.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. Hours are 7 AM to 7 PM Monday to Saturday and 7 AM to 6 PM on Sunday. Booking ahead guarantees your spot for longer styles.

Do you braid kids?

Yes. Kids' styles start at $100, depending on the style.

Ready for your next crown?

Pick your style, see your price and send it to Mimi in under a minute. Walk-ins welcome daily from 7 AM.
